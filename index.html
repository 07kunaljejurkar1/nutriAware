Balanced Plate Ideas

How to construct a balanced meal in a standard college mess.

🌅

Breakfast

Complex carbs + protein (e.g., Poha with peanuts, boiled eggs, or sprout usal).

🍛

Lunch

50% Vegetables/Salad, 25% Dal/Paneer/Egg, 25% Roti or Rice.

🌙

Dinner

Lighter portions with easily digestible items like Khichdi, Dal, and boiled veggies.
